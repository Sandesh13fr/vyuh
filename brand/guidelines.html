Brand toolkit for Vyuh, onchain guardrails for AI trading agents. Seven rings guard one core. Everything in this system explains which ring held and why.

Logo

The mark is two gapped rings around the marigold core: the emblem reduced for small sizes. Use the wordmark where the name is not already on screen.

Do

  • Clear space equal to the core diameter on every side.
  • Minimum mark size 20px; below 64px always use the mark, never the emblem.
  • Keep the core marigold on both themes.

Don't

  • Close the ring gaps, rotate, or add rings to the mark.
  • Recolor the core, add gradients, glows or outlines.
  • Set the wordmark in another face or uppercase.

Color

Restrained: neutrals and indigo do the work. Marigold is the core and one emphasis per view. Held and blocked are states, never decoration. Swatches follow the current theme.

The ring motif

Order is fixed and matches the contract. Every attempt, receipt and mandate can be drawn as these seven rings.

  1. Spend capSPEND_CAP
  2. AllowlistALLOWLIST
  3. Oracle bandORACLE_BAND
  4. SlippageSLIPPAGE
  5. Daily lossDAILY_LOSS
  6. KYC tierKYC_TIER
  7. CooldownCOOLDOWN
held blocked not reached checking now

Motion: an intent travels inward and stops at the ring that held. One authored moment per view; ease-out, no bounce, off under reduced motion.

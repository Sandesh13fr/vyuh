Every intent travels inward. The first ring that fails ends it.

The agent never holds the keys. It emits a structured intent. The vault runs it through seven policy contracts in a fixed order, and only an intent that passes all seven reaches the swap. A failure reverts with the ring and the reason, so you always know why.

  1. 01Spend cap

    Is this trade larger than the per-trade limit?

    ≤ 250.00 USDT per trade

  2. 02Allowlist

    Are both tokens on the owner's list?

    WHSK · USDT · USDC

  3. 03Oracle band

    Is the price within the band of the APRO oracle, and is the oracle fresh?

    ± 1.50% of APRO HSK/USD

  4. 04Slippage

    Does the minimum output allow more slippage than the mandate?

    ≤ 0.50%

  5. 05Daily loss

    Has NAV dropped past the limit since UTC midnight? Then the vault halts.

    ≤ 3.00% of NAV per day

  6. 06KYC tier

    Does the owner hold the required HashKey KYC level?

    level ≥ 2 · ADVANCED

  7. 07Cooldown

    Is it too soon after the last trade?

    ≥ 300 s between trades

  8. The core: the swap runs on HSKSwap V3

    Only after all seven hold. A receipt with the reasoning hash, NAV before and after is indexed for the owner.

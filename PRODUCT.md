# PRODUCT.md

## Product
Vyuh. Onchain guardrails for AI trading agents on HashKey Chain, plus the Arena: a public jailbreak challenge against a real-money agent.

## Mechanism (one sentence)
An LLM proposes a trade as a structured intent; seven onchain policy rings (spend cap, allowlist, oracle band, slippage, daily loss, KYC tier, cooldown) accept or reject it before any token moves.

## Audience and scene
- Hackathon judges and Demo Day crowd in a bright hall in Mumbai, on laptops and phones, scanning a QR to attack the agent.
- Agent builders reading docs and wiring the SDK/MCP at night.
- Retail and institutional owners checking a vault: is it safe, what did the agent do, why was a trade blocked.

## Surfaces
- Landing (Persuade): what Vyuh is, live blocked-attack counter, CTA "Break the Vyuh".
- Arena (Experience): chat with the honeypot agent, live wall of attempts, which ring stopped you.
- Console (Operate): vault, mandate signer, receipts, NAV/P&L, halt.
- Docs (Read).

## Brand commitments
- Name: Vyuh (from Chakravyuh, the seven-ring formation). Tagline: "Agents trade. Vyuh holds."
- The seven rings are the core visual motif.
- Tone: confident, precise, a little defiant. No hype words, no "revolutionary".
- Honest: mocks labelled, targets never shown as results.

## Constraints
- Radix UI primitives, CSS variables, light and dark.
- Must be legible on a projector and on a phone.
